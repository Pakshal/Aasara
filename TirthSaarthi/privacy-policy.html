Temple Guardian · TirthSaarthi

Privacy Policy

Community care for Jain temples and dharmashalas.

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Policy version: 1.2

Temple Guardian (project name: TirthSaarthi) is operated by Pakshal Kumar H Dhelaria. For privacy questions, correction requests or account and content removal requests, contact pakshal94@gmail.com.

This policy explains how the current app handles information when you create an account, report an issue, interact with community reports, view Tirth Rakshaks or submit Tirth Reels.

Public submissions: reports can include photographs, videos, recorded audio, descriptions, precise capture coordinates, location accuracy and dates. Approved reels also display place details and capture location. Other people may view, copy or share public content. Avoid including private addresses, personal contact details, identifiable children or other sensitive information in public submissions.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, account identifier and login/session information. Email/password registration stores a salted password hash, not the original password. If Google sign-in is available and you choose it, we receive the Google account identifier, verified email address and profile name required for sign-in.
  • Reports and places: temple or dharmashala details, issue category, description, photographs, videos and associated audio, timestamps, capture coordinates and location accuracy. A report currently stores one GPS location shared across its attachments.
  • Optional caretaker details: a caretaker or manager name and phone number, if you supply them. Provide these only with permission.
  • Blocking preferences: accounts you block and the time of the block. These settings are private to your account and authorized service administration.
  • Safety reports: the reporting account identifier, reported content or account identifier, selected reason, optional explanation, submission time and moderation status. When you report a contributor, we also retain the related report, comment or reel as evidence. Evidence can include the contributor’s displayed name and a snapshot of the relevant content.
  • Community activity: upvotes, comments, followed reports, notification read states, description corrections, the retained previous description, dated updates and inappropriate-content flags.
  • Tirth Reels: video and recorded audio, tirth name, city, address/how to reach, history/significance, capture location and dates, submission status and review notes.
  • Tirth Rakshaks: names, portraits and descriptions published by administrators.
  • Administration and support: privacy correspondence, moderation reasons, content snapshots, account identifiers and administrative action records. Our hosting and delivery providers may also process connection information such as IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide sign-in, maintain the place directory, submit and display reports and reels, show capture locations, support comments and following, apply your blocking preferences, review safety reports and submissions, respond to support requests, investigate abuse and maintain administrative accountability. The current version does not include advertising or marketing analytics SDKs.

3. What other people can see

  • Public: published place entries, visible reports and their media, descriptions, location and dates; comments and displayed contributor names; dated updates and available description history; Tirth Rakshak profiles; and approved reels with the contributor name, place details, video, capture location and dates.
  • Restricted: account email/phone, optional caretaker contacts, private flags and moderation evidence are restricted to the account holder where applicable and authorized administrative access. Other users do not receive these fields through ordinary public report responses.
  • Unapproved reels: pending and rejected reels are visible to their submitting account and authorized administrators. Review notes are visible to those people. Reels become publicly listed only after admin approval.
  • Administrative records: moderation history and content snapshots are restricted to authorized super administrators.

You can report a report, comment, reel or contributor through the app. Reports of inappropriate content or users are private to authorized super administrators. Blocking hides that person’s reports, reels and comments between the signed-in accounts and prevents report interactions between them. You can view your blocked accounts and unblock them under My reports → Blocked users. Unblocking removes your own block; a block set by the other account still applies. Moderators retain access for review. Blocking does not remove public content for other users, prevent access while signed out or revoke previously issued media links immediately.

Authorized moderators may hide reports, remove comments, unpublish or delete reels, or remove accounts after reviewing submissions. Relevant actions, reasons and evidence are recorded in restricted moderation history. Blocking or reporting someone does not automatically delete their account or content.

4. Permissions and local device storage

We request foreground location to obtain coordinates, accuracy and a timestamp when you use location or camera features. We request camera access to capture report photographs or record videos and microphone access for video audio. Camera capture requires a successful location check before and after recording. We do not implement background location tracking.

Issue reports and reels use camera capture, not a phone-gallery upload option. Authorized super administrators may select a portrait for a Tirth Rakshak profile. You can change permissions in your device settings; features needing a denied permission will not work.

The app stores a login session and interface preferences on your device. Native mobile sessions use secure device storage; the web interface uses browser storage. Captured or cached media may remain on your device according to app or operating-system storage behaviour. Signing out or removing local app data does not itself remove server-side submissions.

5. Service providers and external links

The current API, database and private object storage are hosted on Railway. These services process information needed to operate the app. If you use Google sign-in, Google processes that authentication under its own privacy terms. If you tap a capture-location link, the app opens Google Maps with the selected coordinates; that external service then processes the request under its own privacy policy.

Media is held in private object storage and played through time-limited access links. A previously issued link can remain usable until it expires, including briefly after a reel is unpublished. Public content may also be copied or cached by people who have already viewed it. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or necessary to address a valid safety or security issue.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for network transfers, salted password hashing, authenticated sessions, role-based access controls and private media storage. Access links expire, and moderation operations are recorded. No transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Public submissions are not confidential or end-to-end encrypted. Do not send passwords in support messages.

7. Retention

The current app does not automatically delete reports or reels after 100 days or one year. Account information is kept while an account remains in the service. Published contributions remain until removed through moderation or a supported removal process. Pending and rejected reels remain available to their author and administrators until removed. Incomplete, abandoned reel uploads are eligible for automatic cleanup after 24 hours.

Blocking preferences remain until you unblock the account or either account is removed. Unblocking does not restore notifications removed when the block was applied. Safety reports and their evidence currently have no automatic expiry, including after a review is completed.

Moderation records, including administrator identity, reasons and content snapshots, currently have no automatic expiry. They remain restricted to support accountability and investigations. Removing a report or reel queues its media for deletion; processing can be delayed by storage or network failures. Infrastructure backup and diagnostic-log retention is separate from deletion of live application records.

Any future fixed retention period will be reflected here when adopted and implemented. This policy does not promise a fixed expiry or backup-deletion deadline that the current system does not enforce.

8. Corrections, account removal and privacy requests

Email pakshal94@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. State whether you are requesting account removal, correction or removal of particular content, and identify the relevant report, reel, comment or other entry. Do not include your password. We may request information to verify account ownership before acting.

Current account-removal behaviour: an administrator can remove an account, revoke sessions and delete its profile and votes. Public contributions such as reports, comments, places and reels can remain, with the account link removed. Account identity and relevant content may remain in restricted moderation records. Account removal therefore does not automatically erase all contributions or all historical personal information. If you want personal information removed from specific content or moderation records, identify it in your request so it can be assessed separately.

You can start an account and associated-data deletion request in the app under My reports → Delete account, or use our public account-deletion request page without installing or signing into the app. Requests are sent by email and handled manually; opening the page or email draft does not submit a request or delete an account. Send the email to complete your request. There is currently no automatic processing deadline or fixed expiry for restricted moderation records. Any limited retention required for security, dispute resolution or applicable legal duties will be considered when responding to the request.

9. Other people's information and children

Respect local photography rules and obtain appropriate permission before submitting identifiable people, caretaker information or tribute portraits. Avoid unnecessary personal information, especially information identifying children. If you believe a submission contains a child's information or another person's information that should be removed, contact the support email and identify the content.
